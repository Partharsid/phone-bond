Get more speed

Always worth checking
  • Put phones near a window, standing up and apart from each other.
  • Turn off battery saver and data saver on every phone.
  • Keep phones charging and cool; hot phones slow their modem down.
  • Use USB 3 (blue) ports, and a short, good cable.
  • Phones on different carriers add more than phones on the same one.
  • Check your plan's tethering allowance; some slow down after a daily limit.
